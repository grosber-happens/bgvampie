# Ufuk-i Kehribar (the Amber Horizon) — World Bible

Source of truth (Turkish): `/mnt/project-files/design/dunya-tasarimi.md` in the project files. This is the English brief for the agents working on the repo.
Interactive atlas: https://claude.ai/artifact/AqRB9sb1MZRmUWC27ZKonQ · Class & hero board: https://claude.ai/artifact/3KqLWK5Vj1cdeow7eXvJPC

## Decision (2026-10-29)
The fixed regions (mushroom / ruined city / monkey treetops / desert / red cathedral) are gone. There is **one boundless world**. The old regions survive as **landscapes that blend into each other** with no hard borders.

## Premise
A planet terraformed long ago by "Gardeners": nanite spores to thicken the air, sky-koi bred to eat the spores, and gardener robots that never stopped working. A million-year "Long Quiet" emptied the cities. Now the sun has swollen into a huge orange red giant. Spores in the blood created the **Susuzlar** ("the Thirsty"): nanite-blooded humans who need **Kızıl Öz** (red essence) to live. Vampirism here is a metabolism, not a myth. Players and bots are Susuzlar.

**Match = a Harvest (Hasat).** When the sun "breathes", a **Kızıl Sis** (crimson mist) ring falls on a random slice of the world and closes in. Everyone inside has their essence boiled free; the last one standing drinks it all. Gameplay: each 25–35 min match uses a ~2×2 km slice containing at least 3 landscapes and 1 transition strip; the shrinking ring is the crimson mist.

## Sky (fixed signature)
- Giant bright orange sun near the horizon, about a third of the sky, nearly static: endless golden hour. Core #FF7A1A, halo #FFB347.
- Watercolor sky: peach → salmon → pale turquoise → ink violet at the zenith, paper texture.
- **No clouds: flying Japanese koi** (white/orange, red/black, gold), slow schools, bus-sized up close. A koi shadow briefly lowers mist damage. Optional event "Koi Eclipse": a koi crosses the sun → short noir night.

## Landscapes
| Landscape | Look | Play |
|---|---|---|
| Spor Ormanı (Spore Forest) | tower mushrooms with carved homes, polka-dot ground, moss hills of stone faces, giant pink flowers | vertical play, jump on caps, spore clouds block vision |
| Kemik Şehir (Bone City) | brutalist megastructures, hanging highways, half-buried giant stone heads, rotting neon; noir heart = Gece Karakolu (Night Precinct) | long sightlines, sniper perches |
| Asma Köyler (Hanging Villages) | capsule homes on ropes between giant flat-topped trees, gondolas, zip lines | fast traversal, height advantage |
| Kehribar Kumu (Amber Sands) | orange-pink sand, giant stone hands, clay domes, Gaudí terraces | open ground, monowheel speed, sandstorms |
| Turkuaz Lagün (Turquoise Lagoon) | shallow turquoise water, abandoned concrete villas, waterfall arches | slow in water, shallow water hides footprints |
| Kızıl Kanyon (Crimson Canyon) | snowy blue canyons, giant crimson gates, the Counts' Red Cathedral (its bell foundry casts heavy "Döküm" armor) | common final ring, essence fountains |

## Inhabitants
Frog Guild (armored frog merchants: **where currency is spent** on items, vehicles, tier-3 tokens) · Gardener robots (pot-headed, harmless; hurting them raises Guild prices) · Scribe Insects (violin-playing; sell next-ring info) · Monowheel couriers (**buyable vehicles**) · Sphynx-cat guards · Mushroom Walkers · CRT-headed noir fences · one-eyed moss golems (bosses) · melting shell beasts · syringe mosquitoes (steal essence).

Fixed points in every match slice: Frog Guild shop, monowheel stop, Scribe tower, red-essence fountain (heals but reveals you).

## Playable heroes (hero = look + story, class = kit; any hero can play any class)
Mina "Kâkül" (human, ex-courier of the Night Precinct; black bob, pink headphones) · Gurr (armored giant frog, runaway Guild debt collector) · Seraf (tattooed sphynx cat, ex-guard) · Ilgın (renegade scribe insect, nano wing) · Nene Döküm (old foundry master, self-cast mechanical arm) · Teker (monowheel courier; companion wolf "Pas").

## Visual language
Moebius clean even ink lines × Kim Jung Gi energy × Karl Kopinski weathered machinery; futuristic noir (flat ink-violet shadows, one warm light source). 3D: characters and buildings clay, sky and VFX watercolor (already approved).
Palette: sun #FF7A1A, amber #FFB347, koi red #E0412B, bone #F2E8D5, turquoise #3FB8AF, spore pink #F06EA9, moss #8DBF6A, ink violet #2B1E3F. UI tokens shared with the items/UI work: paper #F3EAD7, ink #15131A, amber #F2B33D, teal #4FB3BF, rose #E2475C.
Class signature colors: Rogue #E0412B, Mage #4FB3BF, Fighter #FF7A1A, Scout #8DBF6A.
